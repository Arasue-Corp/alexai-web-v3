What exactly is Alex AI Insurtech?

Alex AI is a hybrid insurance platform that merges predictive artificial intelligence with expert human empathy. Unlike traditional carriers, our Neural Engine analyzes thousands of data points to generate hyper-personalized coverage in seconds, eliminating paperwork and reducing premiums by cutting administrative inefficiencies.

How does Alex AI work?

Our intelligent AI analyzes your profile and needs to find your perfect insurance match. In just a few clicks, you'll receive customized options. Forget the paperwork and the phone calls—this is instant protection.

What types of coverage do you offer?

We provide a comprehensive suite of protection products:
• Auto: Liability, collision, and comprehensive with telematics options.
• Homeowners & Renters: Structure and contents protection.
• Tech & Cyber: Coverage for devices and digital identity.
• Commercial: Protect your company and your assets from any risk.
All policies can be bundled for maximum efficiency and savings.

Is my personal data secure?

Security is our foundation. We utilize military-grade AES-256 encryption for all data storage and transmission. Our infrastructure is SOC2 Type II compliant, meaning your personal and financial information is fortified by the strictest data privacy protocols in the industry.

What makes Alex AI different?

It's simple: we put you in control. Instant quotes powered by AI, transparent pricing with no hidden fees, 24/7 real-time support, and full control to manage your policy anytime, anywhere.

Can I cancel or modify my policy?

You have total control. Via the Command Center (App or Web), you can add drivers, change vehicles, adjust limits, or cancel your policy instantly. No phone calls, no retention agents, no hassle.

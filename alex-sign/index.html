What is Alex Sign

Alex Sign is an electronic signature platform that allows you to sign documents with full legal validity, from any device and at any time. It is designed for both individual users and businesses looking to digitize and automate their signing.

What makes Alex Sign different?

Alex Sign combines legal e-signature technology, artificial intelligence, and automation. Key features include AI-Powered generation of legal documents, bulk signing, and advanced security logs.

Can I automatically generate documents?

Yes. Alex Sign includes an AI-Powered document generator that helps you create personalized contracts, agreements, and other legal documents in minutes.

Is it legal to sign with Alex Sign?

Yes. All electronic signatures made through Alex Sign comply with U.S. laws such as the ESIGN Act and UETA, making them fully legally binding.

How does bulk signing work??

Bulk signing allows you to send and sign multiple documents at once, whether for different recipients or repeated workflows. Ideal for HR or sales teams.

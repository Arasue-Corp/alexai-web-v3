1. Introduction

Alex AI Insurtech, LLC ("AlexAI," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website and use our services, including our online comparative insurance rating platform (collectively, the "Services").

Agreement & Compliance

By accessing our Services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy is in compliance with applicable federal laws and the Arizona Revised Statutes, including ARS § 18-545 and ARS § 44-7601.

2. Types Of Information We Collect

Visitors

When you visit our website or request information, we collect provided personal details and navigational data.

Personal Info

Name, email, address, phone, transaction details, and publicly available info (Facebook, LinkedIn, Google).

Third Party

Information acquired from partners, co-branded services, joint marketing efforts, and social media platforms.

Navigational

IP address, browser type, geo-location, search terms, pages visited, and email interactions (opens/clicks).

Log Files

We automatically gather computer hardware/software info (IP, ISP, OS, clickstream) to generate usage statistics. We link this data to your Personal Information.

Personal Information

We may use cookies/beacons to deliver relevant ads across different websites based on your visits. We track ad effectiveness.

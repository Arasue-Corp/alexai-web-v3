What does homeowners insurance typically cover?

Coverage usually includes:
• The structure of your home (walls, roof, floor, etc.).
• Personal belongings inside the home.
• Liability (injuries or property damage to others).
• Additional living expenses if your home becomes unlivable.
• Optional protections (flood, earthquake, umbrella policies, etc.).
All policies can be bundled for maximum efficiency and savings.

Is homeowners insurance required?

If you have a mortgage, yes—your lender will require it. Even if you own your home outright, it's highly recommended to protect your asset from unexpected disasters.

Why get insurance through Alex AI?

We shop around for you. We pull quotes from top-rated insurers and help you compare coverage, pricing, and features so you get the best deal with less effort.

Can I bundle home and auto?

Definitely. Many of our partner insurers offer bundle discounts, and Alex AI automatically checks for those to help you save even more.
